Welcome to Britestar

Britestar provides a safe, supportive after-school environment for children. We help kids learn, play, build confidence and friendships — while giving parents peace of mind.

We offer structured activities, creative play, and homework time in a warm and welcoming space.

Our aim is to create an engaging environment that balances fun and learning, designed around the needs of each child.

Children enjoy a mix of active play, creative activities, quiet time, and support with homework — all supervised by caring staff.

We work closely with families to build a positive community where every child feels safe, included, and encouraged to grow.

Welcome message

Welcome to Britestar Kidz Club! We are a fun, safe, and caring Before and After School Programme based at Holy Cross Catholic School in Henderson. Our goal is to provide a supportive environment where children can relax, play, learn and grow outside of school hours. At Britestar Kidz Club, we focus on building friendships, encouraging creativity, and giving every child a place where they feel happy and valued. Whether it’s starting the day with energy or winding down after school, we are here to support your child every step of the way. We look forward to welcoming you and your family
