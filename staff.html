Meet Our Team

At Britestar, our team is the heart of everything we do. Each member brings passion, experience, and a genuine commitment to creating a safe, engaging, and supportive environment for every child in our care. We believe that before and after school care should be more than supervision - it should be a space where children feel valued, encouraged, and inspired to grow. From creative activities to active play and homework support, our staff work together to make every day meaningful and fun. Our educators and leaders are carefully selected for their dedication, energy, and ability to connect with children of all ages. We pride ourselves on building strong relationships with families and fostering a positive community where everyone feels welcome. Get to know the friendly faces who make Britestar a place children love to be.
