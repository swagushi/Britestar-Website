Our Services

We are a fun, safe, and caring Before and After School Programme based at Holy Cross Catholic School in Henderson. Our goal is to provide a supportive environment where children can relax, play, learn and grow outside of school hours. At Britestar Kidz Club, we focus on building friendships, encouraging creativity, and giving every child a place where they feel happy and valued. Whether it’s starting the day with energy or winding down after school, we are here to support your child every step of the way. We look forward to welcoming you and your family

Our Who we are

At Britestar Kidz Club Holy Cross Catholic School, we are a dedicated Before and After School Care Programme. We are passionate about creating a safe, welcoming, and fun environment where every child feels supported and cared for. Our team of trained and friendly staff are committed to looking after your children with the highest level of care and attention. During our programmes, children are supervised at all times, ensuring their safety while they enjoy a range of activities arranged for each term of learning. We also provide healthy, nutritious food to keep children energised and happy throughout their time with us. At Britestar Kidz Club, we believe in nurturing both the wellbeing and development of every child in our care. Why do we do this?

Britestar provides a safe, supportive after-school environment for children. We help kids learn, play, build confidence and friendships — while giving parents peace of mind. We offer structured activities, creative play, and homework time in a warm and welcoming space. Our aim is to create an engaging environment that balances fun and learning, designed around the needs of each child. Children enjoy a mix of active play, creative activities, quiet time, and support with homework — all supervised by caring staff. We work closely with families to build a positive community where every child feels safe, included, and encouraged to grow.
